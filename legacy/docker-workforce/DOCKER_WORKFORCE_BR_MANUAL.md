# DOCKER WORKFORCE — MANUAL TÉCNICO, OPERACIONAL E COMERCIAL (PT-BR / EN)
## Enterprise Workforce Intelligence & Payroll Compliance for Brazil (CLT, eSocial, LGPD)

### 1. Visão Geral / Overview
Docker Workforce (Verdant Green Edition) is an independent, enterprise-grade SaaS platform designed to orchestrate complex 24/7 workforce operations across Brazil (including regional nuances from Recife and Aracaju to São Paulo and Porto Alegre). It guarantees strict compliance with CLT and eSocial (events S-1200, S-2299), backed by an immutable SHA-256 audit ledger, 3-node quorum cryptographic consensus, and autonomous AI validation.

---

### 2. Módulos Principais / Core Modules
1. **CLT & eSocial Compliance Engine**: Automated calculation of overtime (horas extras), night shift differential (adicional noturno), inter/intrajornada breaks, and eSocial reporting hooks.
2. **3-Node Quorum Consensus Protocol**: Critical operations require cryptographic confirmation (ECDSA/Ed25519) from at least 2 of 3 independent nodes (Primary, Validator, Witness) before execution.
3. **Heavy Audit Trail (Ledger Encadeado)**: Append-only event store with SHA-256 hash chaining, signed snapshots, and automatic divergence detection.
4. **Absence Management**: 16+ Brazilian absence types (férias, licença médica, licença maternidade/paternidade) with direct impact on coverage calculations and pre-folha computation.
5. **Pre-Folha Payroll Ledger**: Automated pre-calculation with retroactive recalculation capability and bank export formats.
